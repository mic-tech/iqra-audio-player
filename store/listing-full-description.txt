IQRA Audio Player plays a folder of audio files from beginning to end, in order.

Point it at a folder on your device and every audio file inside it, including everything in its sub-folders, becomes one continuous playlist. There are no playlists to build, no library to import, and no account to create.

BUILT FOR FOLDER-ORGANISED AUDIO

Ideal for Quran recitations, audiobooks, lecture series, language courses, and anything else that arrives as a numbered set of files. If the order of the files matters, this player keeps it.

ORDERING THAT MAKES SENSE

Tracks are ordered by folder first, then by file name, and numbers are compared as numbers. Track 2 comes before Track 10, and Disc 2 before Disc 10, instead of the jumbled order plain alphabetical sorting gives you.

THE CONTROLS YOU EXPECT

- A scrubbable seek bar with elapsed and remaining time
- Previous, next, and ten-second skip in both directions
- Keeps playing in the background, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen
- Works with Bluetooth headsets and car stereos
- Pauses automatically when you unplug your headphones
- An optional repeat mode that loops the whole folder

PICKS UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF

Close the app and come back tomorrow: it resumes on the same track, at the same position. Your repeat setting is remembered too. On a shared device, that means the next person starts where the last one stopped.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

The app does not request internet access, so it is incapable of sending anything anywhere. There are no advertisements, no analytics, no tracking, and no sign-in. The only things it saves are the folder you chose, your playback position, and your repeat setting, and all of it stays on your device.

READS YOUR FILES, CHANGES NOTHING

Choosing a folder grants read-only access to that folder alone. The app never modifies, moves, deletes, or copies your files, and it cannot see anything outside the folder you picked.

FORMATS

MP3 is the focus. M4A, M4B, AAC, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, WAV, WMA and MKA play too.

OPEN SOURCE

The complete source code, including the manifest listing every permission the app requests, is published at github.com/mic-tech/iqra-audio-player
