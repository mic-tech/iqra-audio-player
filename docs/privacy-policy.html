Privacy Policy

IQRA Audio Player (com.maryumcenter.iqraaudioplayer) — published by Maryum Islamic Center.

Last updated: 9 September 2026

In short: this app collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet access at all. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

No data is collected

IQRA Audio Player does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device information. There is no sign-in, no user account, and no server operated by us that the app talks to.

This is enforced technically, not just by policy: the app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it is incapable of sending data anywhere, to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally so it can resume where you left off. This never leaves your device and is not readable by us.

WhatWhy
The folder you choseSo the app reopens your library without asking again
Current track and playback positionTo resume where you stopped
Repeat settingTo remember your preference
A cached list of the audio file names found in your folderSo the app starts quickly instead of rescanning every time

All of this is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you clear its data from Android Settings.

Access to your files

When you choose a folder, Android's system folder picker grants the app read-only access to that folder and the folders inside it. The app uses this solely to find and play audio files. It does not modify, delete, upload, or copy your files, and it has no access to anything outside the folder you picked.

Permissions the app requests

PermissionPurpose
NotificationsTo show the playback notification with play, pause and skip controls
Foreground service (media playback)To keep audio playing when the app is in the background or the screen is off
Wake lockTo prevent the device sleeping mid-track during playback
Network stateDeclared automatically by the Android media playback library the app is built on. The app performs no network activity and cannot, as it has no internet permission.

No advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party software development kits that gather information about you. It does not access the Android advertising identifier.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. As described above, the app collects no information from any user regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date above. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to tech@maryumcenter.com.

IQRA Audio Player is open source. The source code, including the manifest that lists every permission described above, is available at github.com/mic-tech/iqra-audio-player.
